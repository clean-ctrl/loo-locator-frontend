    Meet the fish

    Every print is a local.

    Our designs start with the species that swim right off our shores. Wear one and you'll probably end up explaining salmon life cycles at a party. We're okay with that.

    Coho Salmon

    Silver in the ocean, brick-red when they come home. Coho return to the tiny creeks of the Saanich Peninsula every fall.

    Chinook

    The big one. The Southern Resident orcas' favourite meal, and ours to protect.

    Rockfish

    Spiky, grumpy-looking and can live over 100 years. Honestly, relatable.

    Pacific Herring

    Small fish, big deal. Their spring spawn turns the coast milky-turquoise and feeds almost everything.

    5% for streams

    Buy a fish. Help a fish.

    5% of our profits go straight back to local conservation, including the work of Peninsula Streams Society — the folks restoring creeks, shorelines and salmon habitat around the Saanich Peninsula.

    • Restoring healthy streams and shorelines
    • Protecting salmon spawning habitat
    • Caring for whole watersheds, from headwaters to the sea

    Our story

    Born on the coast, a little bit fishy.

    Big Fish started with a simple idea: the fleece you grab on a cold, misty Victoria morning should be as fun as the ocean outside your door. So we covered one in fish.

    Every design is inspired by the Pacific Northwest species that swim right off our shores — the ones we cheer on every salmon run. Cozy enough for a winter dock, bright enough to spot across the ferry deck.

    — Big Fish Apparel, Victoria BC
